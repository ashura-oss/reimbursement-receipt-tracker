These embedded PDF fonts are static regular faces generated from the official Google Fonts variable fonts at weight 400 (Noto Sans and Noto Sans Tamil; width 100 for Noto Sans) and distributed under their included SIL Open Font License 1.1 texts.

Sources:
- Noto Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosans
- Noto Sans Simplified Chinese: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
- Noto Sans Tamil: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanstamil

Noto Sans SC and Tamil are used for Chinese and Tamil respectively. Unsupported Unicode characters, including emoji without a bundled glyph, are shown as explicit [U+XXXX] code-point text in generated PDFs rather than being silently dropped.
